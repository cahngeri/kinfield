# Phase-based Campaign & Content Development

## Overview
Parenting is divided into distinct, non-linear phases. A campaign that resonates with an expectant mother in her third trimester is irrelevant to a mother dealing with a toddler's sleep regression. 

KINFIELD designs **Phase-based Campaigns** that synchronize brand messaging with the exact developmental stage of both child and parent.

## The Lifecycle Stages
1. **The Expecting Phase (Pregnancy)**: High research intent, nursery preparation, anxiety regarding birth and infant safety.
2. **The 4th Trimester / Newborn Phase (0–3 Months)**: Intensive vulnerability, physical recovery, nocturnal feeding, radical need for gentle, hypoallergenic care.
3. **The Infant & Explorer Phase (4–12 Months)**: Teething, weaning, motor development, exploration, babyproofing.
4. **The Toddler Phase (1–3 Years)**: Independence, outdoor activities, tantrums, cognitive play, apparel durability.

## Key Deliverables
- Lifecycle funnel mapping and automated sequence recommendations.
- Empathy-driven seasonal and milestone-based campaign toolkits.
- Creative assets (stills, carousels, video scripts) mapped to specific parenting phases.
