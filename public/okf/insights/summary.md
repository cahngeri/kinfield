# KINFIELD Research Perspectives & Market Insights

## Key Published Articles

### 1. Why 85% of Parents Read Reviews Before Buying (March 2026)
- **Core Finding**: In high-stakes categories like baby care, the information search phase is prolonged. Over 85% of parents read reviews to de-risk purchases.
- **Strategic Takeaway**: Brands must prioritize review collection, post-purchase unboxing workflows, and transparent response mechanisms over aggressive top-of-funnel reach.
- **Reference URL**: https://kinfield.id/insight/why-85-percent-parents-read-reviews

### 2. Community Seeding: The Most Underused Strategy in Baby Brands (February 2026)
- **Core Finding**: Peer-to-peer discussions on WhatsApp groups, school pickup circles, and neighborhood forums exert an outsized influence on brand trial.
- **Strategic Takeaway**: Seeding product to genuine micro-influencer mothers who share daily parenting realities produces 3x higher long-term brand equity than traditional celebrity billboards.
- **Reference URL**: https://kinfield.id/insight/community-seeding-baby-brands

### 3. Phase-Based Marketing: Meeting Parents Where They Are (January 2026)
- **Core Finding**: A parent’s priorities shift radically every 3 to 6 months in lockstep with child development milestones.
- **Strategic Takeaway**: Segmenting communications chronologically (Expectant, Newborn, Explorer, Toddler) reduces customer acquisition cost (CAC) and dramatically elevates customer lifetime value (LTV).
- **Reference URL**: https://kinfield.id/insight/phase-based-marketing

### 4. The Reassurance Economy: Marketing Beyond Persuasion (December 2025)
- **Core Finding**: The modern parenting market has transitioned from persuasion-driven buying to the "Reassurance Economy."
- **Strategic Takeaway**: Parents seek relief from decision fatigue and safety anxiety. Brands that present themselves as trusted partners rather than aggressive salesmen win enduring market share.
- **Reference URL**: https://kinfield.id/insight/reassurance-economy
