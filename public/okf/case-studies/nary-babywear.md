# Case Study: Nary Babywear — Brand & KOL Activation

## Client & Challenge
- **Client**: Nary Babywear
- **Category**: Premium baby and infant apparel
- **The Challenge**: Facing high competition from budget babywear marketplaces, Nary needed to defend its premium price point by communicating superior tactile comfort, non-irritating fabric quality, and emotional bonding.

## Strategic Approach
1. **Insight**: Parents will happily invest in premium apparel if they believe it protects their baby's delicate skin and provides emotional comfort during sleep and play.
2. **KOL Anchor Partnership**: Strategic collaboration with celebrity parent and trusted mother figure **Mona Ratuliu** (@monaratuliu).
3. **Lifestyle Storytelling**: Shifted from catalog-style flat lays to intimate lifestyle vignettes capturing spontaneous moments between mother and baby at home.
4. **Community Co-creation Campaign**: An interactive challenge inviting everyday parents to share their baby’s comforting nursery moments.

## Verified Results & Impact
- **Audience Growth**: **+3,200 organic followers** acquired in a 30-day campaign burst.
- **Campaign Participation**: Over **200+ parents** actively submitted high-quality UGC during the activation.
- **Sustained Scale**: **2.5x quarterly follower growth** and sustained commercial sell-through.
- **Brand Sentiment**: **90% positive sentiment** across community comments and direct inquiries.
