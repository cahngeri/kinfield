# Case Study: Bebio — Telon Skincare Campaign

## Client & Challenge
- **Client**: Bebio (Indonesian baby care brand)
- **Category**: Baby skincare & traditional telon oil
- **The Challenge**: In Indonesia, telon oil is historically treated as a situational remedy—only applied when an infant is cold, bloated, or bitten by insects. Bebio needed to differentiate in an entrenched market dominated by legacy brands and elevate telon into a modern, essential daily skincare routine.

## Strategic Approach
KINFIELD conceptualized and executed the **#TelonSkincareBebio** movement:
1. **Insight**: Modern millennial mothers actively practice skincare for themselves and seek gentle, nourishing rituals for their babies that go beyond basic warmth.
2. **Repositioning**: Redefined Bebio Telon as an everyday hydration and calming ritual after each bath.
3. **Execution**:
   - Educational content dismantling common newborn skincare myths.
   - Micro-influencer mom seeding showcasing real bedtime bonding routines.
   - Clean, aesthetic packaging styling highlighting natural essential oils.

## Verified Results & Impact
- **Engagement Lift**: **3x organic engagement growth** across social media platforms.
- **Audience Sentiment**: **85% positive sentiment** generated directly from organic UGC and customer reviews.
- **Market Perception**: Successfully established Bebio as the pioneer of the "Telon Skincare" category in the minds of young Indonesian mothers.
