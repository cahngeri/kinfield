## Core Philosophy: Reassurance Over Persuasion
Traditional consumer marketing relies heavily on urgency, impulse triggers, and transactional discounts. KINFIELD is founded on the empirical understanding that parents behave as **protectors** rather than conventional consumers:
- Over 85% of parents consult online reviews before buying children's products.
- 88% bypass conventional advertising in favor of genuine community recommendations (Nielsen).
- Parents require transparent ingredient lists, pediatric safety confirmations, and peer proof rather than aggressive sales tactics.
